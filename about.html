About GameMatch

GameMatch is a class project designed to help Steam players spend less time choosing and more time playing.

Why GameMatch?

Having plenty of games to choose from can make deciding difficult. GameMatch aims to narrow those choices to a short list that fits your time, company, mood, and preferred level of effort.

The project

Built for CM523, GameMatch explores accessible, responsive web design and understandable game recommendations.

The planned recommendations draw from a curated collection, organized around four moods: relaxing, energetic, immersive, and competitive.
