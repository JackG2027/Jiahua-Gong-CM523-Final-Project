How GameMatch works

Four short questions help narrow the choices to games that fit what you want to play right now.

Tell us what fits

  1. Time: How long do you have to play?
  2. Who: Are you playing alone or with others?
  3. Mood: Do you want something relaxing, energetic, immersive, or competitive?
  4. Effort: How much focus do you want to give?

You can go back and change your answers as you move through the quiz.

Explore your recommendations

Your answers narrow a curated game collection to up to three suggestions, with explanations of why they fit.

Open a game's details to learn more, then follow its Steam link when you are ready to explore further.
